### 🤖 Claude

Both, but for different reasons — and H5 is really three separate things with very different value.

## E7 — yes, clearly worth it

It lands on the marquee beat. [DEMO.md](docs/DEMO.md) Beat 3 publishes the AI-built theme at ~4:30, Beat 4 opens the shop at 5:30. The staleness is up to ~2 min (60s `revalidate` in [shop.ts](apps/storefront/src/lib/shop.ts) *plus* 60s `s-maxage` in [constants.ts](packages/config/src/constants.ts)), so whether the theme is fresh at 5:30 is roughly a coin flip.

The script already concedes this in two places — a "Timing note: keep talking, and it will be live" in Beat 3, and a troubleshooting entry: *"The storefront still shows the old theme. Wait and reload."* And "reload once" doesn't reliably fix it: if the presenter reloads with 30s left on the cache, they reload into the same old theme and have to stall again. That's the "your publish is broken" moment on the beat that's supposed to land hardest.

Size S, and it lets you delete both mitigations from the script plus shrink H2 flow (d)'s 90s poll.

## H5 — worth it, but only one of the three parts is about the demo proper

I checked each against the script:

**1. Seeded app + webhook + delivery log — the real win.** Beat 0 currently has the presenter hand-create an app, tick a scope, add *two* webhook subscriptions, and dismiss three one-time secret dialogs — all before the clock starts, all of it load-bearing for Beat 7. That's the most failure-prone prep in the script (the troubleshooting section already has an entry for it). Seeding it collapses that to just starting the echo receiver. Do this one.

**2. Jane's orders and 3. abandoned checkouts — off-script, but on clickable surfaces.** Neither appears in the 14 minutes: customer accounts aren't a beat, and Beat 2's Customers stop only points at order counts and lifetime spend. But both are one click from where the demo goes — "Abandoned checkouts" is a permanent 4th tab at [customers/page.tsx:41](apps/admin/src/app/store/[slug]/customers/page.tsx:41), and the storefront header links `/account` at [storefront-header.tsx:45](apps/storefront/src/components/storefront-header.tsx:45). They render correct, honest empty states — the risk isn't a crash, it's a prospect in Q&A clicking one and reading "unbuilt."

That's Q&A insurance, not demo path. Cheap enough (it's all in `packages/db/prisma/seed/`) that I'd do it in the same PR, but I wouldn't hold the demo for it.

**Recommendation:** E7 and H5 together are maybe an afternoon. Both S. E7 for the demo path, H5's app-seed for the prep, H5's other two as insurance. C7 (filter pills on an index the demo visits in Beat 2) is comparably cheap. E6 is the one I'd leave — it's M-sized, needs a design call, and the demo's saved-card beat is an optional pocket beat that already works against seeded data.

Want me to take E7 and H5?
